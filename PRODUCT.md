# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, faculty, prospective collaborators, recruiters, and students who need a concise, trustworthy view of Yeqi Huang's research, publications, projects, background, and contact information in English or Chinese.

## Product Purpose

Maintain an accurate bilingual academic portfolio that makes current research contributions easy to scan and gives visitors direct access to publications, projects, and the latest CV.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and research-first. The site should communicate technical depth and professional confidence without promotional exaggeration.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic commercial landing-page patterns, decorative visual noise, exaggerated marketing claims, and dense interfaces that compete with the academic content. Preserve the established personal identity instead of introducing trend-driven redesigns.

## Design Principles

- Keep publication status, titles, authorship, and results consistent across the homepage and both CVs.
- Put research content and evidence before decoration.
- Make bilingual navigation and content parity predictable.
- Keep important information easy to scan on desktop and mobile.
- Preserve the site's established visual identity unless a redesign is explicitly requested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic page structure, readable contrast and type sizes, keyboard-accessible controls, responsive layouts, clear link labels, dark/light theme support, and reduced-motion-safe behavior for any future animation.
